How does aging affect our body's ability to perform during exercise?

Data

Our data was extracted from the Treadmill Maximal Exercise Tests from the Exercise Physiology and Human Performance Lab of the University of Malaga dataset from PhysioNet's Open Databases. In this dataset, each athlete performed a maximal Graded Exercise Testing (GET) on a PowerJog J series treadmill connected to a CPX MedGraphics gas analyzer system with breath-by-breath measurements of key cardiac and respiratory parameters used in sport science and medicine and heart rate was collected by a Mortara 12-lead ECG device. The athletes were asked to run on a continuous (ramping) or step-by-step incremental effort on the treadmill until beyond exhaustion. Most of the exercise phases are preceded by a warmup period of walking at 5 km/h. The test was considered maximal if the oxygen consumption of the participant was saturated (VO2 starts plateauing). Ou visualizations will group participants into 6 age groups as seen below and aggregates their average measurements across each age group.
