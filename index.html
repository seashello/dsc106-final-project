    Write up

    Currently, we cleaned the data for our project and we used it to create 2 interactive bar charts. Our project aims to visualize the difference in cardiac and respiratory performances across different age groups. We aggregated our data into 6 age groups from (10, 20] to (60, 70] and created 6 people figures on our webpage to represent each age group. We also have a live scoreboard which will be updated with “real-time” announcements of which age group has reached exhaustion and their measurement values at that moment. The bars in the bar graphs below will also update according to when each age group reaches exhaustion. Currently, we created placeholders for all these components but we will soon sync them and include animations.
    The most challenging part of this project may be the syncing step. We will need to ensure everything on the page works together and accurately. We also think the animation step can be a challenge since everything will now “auto-update” and is no longer controlled by user interaction. This will likely cause the syncing of components to take a little more time as well.
